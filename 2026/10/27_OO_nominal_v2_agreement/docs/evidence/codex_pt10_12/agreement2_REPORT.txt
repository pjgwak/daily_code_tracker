ACCEPTANCE AUDIT
PR: Gaussian CDF integrals weighted with full-space physical Gaussian fractions.
NP: reconstructed decay PDF after convolution with the full-space prompt resolution; native truth-window yields are transported to full space.
The integration denominator is the full real line, not the fit-normalized truncated PDF.
NP integrals are evaluated with RooDecay and checked independently with analytic exGaussian CDFs.
Reconstructed in-window mixture weights are compared to saved PR/NP transport fractions.
Max CDF-vs-RooFit error = 4.108e-15; max saved-weight reconstruction error = 1.11e-16.
Both prefit and postfit coverages are evaluated at fitted central parameter values, not a confidence bound.
